# R2-D1-HOLD-003 — One-variable underwater-held-position trim (preregistered)

**Date:** 2026-11-04. **State:** RESEARCH_HYPOTHESIS, NOT FINANCIALLY TESTED, NOT PRODUCT APPROVED.

## Causal observation behind this experiment

HOLD-001 with new-entry freezing had severe compounding damage; independently audited HOLD-002 removed that freeze but showed limited protection and a worse maximum drawdown in the synthetic rotation regime. Blind reductions of all held coins during a portfolio drawdown can sell positions with positive individual unrealized returns. New rule *ablation*: keep the exact HOLD-002 risk episode, trigger thresholds, native next-open execution, no-entry-freeze, unchanged rearm, quantities, execution, fee/costbasis and slots; change only which holdings are eligible for a protective trim.

## Frozen HOLD-003 policy before its first economic run

- Same original frozen product `f56d48536525830dd6ea50bf5bc174a6f9aeff8c`, same original Input-Contract.json, normal/stress costs, initial capital, native executed fills, and two-slot accounting as HOLD-002.
- Trigger once after **72 consecutive closed hourly portfolio equity samples at 15% or more below the global HWM**. Keep the single defensive episode state to prevent hourly repeated sells. No quarter reset.
- At trigger freeze **25% of the regular position quantity** for every held coin (dust excluded), as HOLD-002 does. The SELL execution is attempted once at the immediately following native candle open **after ordinary native exits** and before new entries. Do not retry failed min-notional, missing-position or other filtered sells.
- **Single modified selection predicate:** Before placing the candidate sale, use only the coin's last **closed** hourly candle at the trigger (not next bar open) and the position's still-owned weighted aggregate cost basis as of that same decision. Compute estimated net liquidation value of all held units at the known last close after the already frozen model's adverse execution-rate and exit fee: `q * last_closed_price * (1 - adverse_price_rate) * (1 - fee_rate)`. A held position is **underwater** if and only if this value is *strictly less* than the recorded remaining total cost basis. If not underwater, skip the research trim and log `TRIM_SKIPPED_NOT_UNDERWATER`. For a native regular exit that has already removed the position, native exit wins. The estimate determines eligibility only; actual sale uses unchanged native *next candle open* and actual costs.
- No per-coin ranking or discretionary coins. Each eligible coin receives at most one 25%-quantity attempt in the episode, rounded and filtered by native market rules. No forced buyback. Original native entry gate remains fully active. After 168 contiguous closed hours of portfolio drawdown **strictly below 10%**, clear defensive state; a later separate eligible drawdown episode can trigger again.
- Maintain exact native proportional lot cost basis on partial fills, fees once, slippage and minimum order quantities. Existing slots remain occupied after partial reduction. SUI/UNI remain research-only and 15 monitored markets do not imply 15 active profiles.
